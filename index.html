China Salt Flow & Consumption

China stands as the leading market destination for raw salt exported from India. Inside China, imports are heavily directed into highly demanding industrial sectors:

Salt Destination in China Estimated Share of Total Salt Consumption
Chlor-alkali industry (PVC, caustic soda, chlorine) 60–70%
Glass production 10–15%
Other chemical industries 10–15%
Food-grade salt (human consumption) 3–5%
Livestock, water treatment, and other uses 5–10%

Salt Consumption & Imports in China

In the most recent 2026 data, India accounted for approximately 78% of China's raw salt imports, reinforcing its position as China's leading supplier of imported salt.

Salt Destination in China Estimated Share of Total Salt Consumption
Chlor-alkali industry (PVC, caustic soda, chlorine) 60–70%
Glass production 10–15%
Other chemical industries 10–15%
Food-grade salt (human consumption) 3–5%
Livestock, water treatment, and other uses 5–10%

Summary: China purchases between 50% and 80% of its imported salt from India, depending on the year and the specific salt category analyzed. According to the latest available figures, India's share is about 78%.
